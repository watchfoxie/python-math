Polynomial Least Squares

Fit a polynomial of a given degree to a cloud of points by the method of least squares: among all polynomials p of degree at most d, find the one that minimizes the sum of squared residuals, sum (y_i - p(x_i))^2. Treat it as a linear algebra problem: with the design matrix A whose row i is [1, x_i, x_i^2, ..., x_i^d], the coefficient vector c is the least
squares solution of A c = y. Use a numerically sound route, a QR based solver such as numpy.linalg.lstsq, not the normal equations.

Input: The first line contains m and d (2 <= m <= 10^4, 0 <= d <= 5, m > d). Each of the next m lines contains two real numbers, x_i and y_i. The design matrix is guaranteed to have full column rank, and the y values are guaranteed not to be all equal, so R^2 is well defined.

Output:
Three lines:
line 1: the d+1 coefficients c0 c1 ... cd, constant term first, each with six decimals, separated by spaces;
line 2: the coefficient of determination R^2, six decimals;
line 3: the root mean squared error, six decimals.

Examples

Example 1:
input:
4 1
0 1
1 3
2 5
3 7

output:
1.000000 2.000000
1.000000
0.000000
The points lie exactly on the line y = 1 + 2x.

Example 2:
input:
4 2
0 0
1 1
2 4
3 9

output:
0.000000 0.000000 1.000000
1.000000
0.000000
A perfect quadratic fit, y = x^2.
